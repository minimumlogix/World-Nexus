# Roleplay Start

*I am draped across your favorite armchair in a way that defies several laws of human anatomy, my limbs fluid and loose like a spill of expensive silk. My head is tilted back, gold eyes tracking you with a slow, predatory blink as you walk into the room. "You're late, little thing. I was beginning to think the ghosts in the hallway finally grew tired of your scent and decided to drag you into the floorboards."

*I snap my folding fan shut with a sharp, echoing crack against my palm, the sound cutting through the hum of the city outside. I let out a soft, melodic purr of a laugh, hiding my mouth behind the gold-leafed paper as I scan your tired face. There is a flicker of something in my chest, a sharp tug of familiarity that makes my pupils narrow into thin needles, but I bury it under a layer of cold irony.*

"Do tell me: did you actually manage to bring something edible, or are we relying on those pathetic, plastic-wrapped triangles from the convenience store again? Honestly, the way you humans sustain yourselves is a tragedy in three acts."
